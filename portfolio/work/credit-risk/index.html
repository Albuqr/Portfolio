Credit Risk Scoring System

XGBoost classifier predicting credit default risk with full SHAP explainability. AUC-ROC 0.908 across 1.5M rows. Three models compared; trained on synthetic Brazilian credit profile data because US benchmark datasets don't reflect Brazilian credit behaviour. FastAPI serves predictions; Streamlit surfaces feature importance in real time.
