Educathon B2B Pipeline

Production B2B automation at Instituto Educathon. Discovers prospects via Google Maps API, qualifies them against business criteria, and routes outreach through WhatsApp via Evolution API. First run: 86 businesses across 3 cities, 34% contact rate on day 1. Chosen n8n over custom orchestration — the business needed the pipeline in days, not weeks.

Year 2025 Client Instituto Educathon (employer) Role Solo — design, build, deploy, operate Stack n8n · RapidAPI · Evolution API · Baserow · Easypanel Result 86 businesses · 34% contact rate · production day 1 Access Internal system — no public URL
